## New paper and version policy

The Google share link `https://share.google/ZWCaJFEKR7X3wjbPG` resolves to arXiv 2608.05797, *Predicting Task Difficulty Without Rollouts*, by Stefan Krsteski and Charlotte Meyer. The saved PDF is explicitly version 3 (2026-10-15); the arXiv citation export supplies year 2026.

Official arXiv exports sometimes use the revised-version year. In particular, Zhang et al. first submitted their paper in 2025, but its v2 is dated 2026-07-21 and the current export uses 2026. Scales++ was first submitted in 2025; its current export uses 2026. These exported years are retained here. The existing paper bibliography uses `zhang2025benchmark` and year 2025: that is a first-submission convention, not a different paper. Do not create duplicate citations when copying entries into the manuscript bibliography.

Published PMLR entries are used for CNP, GPIRT, MPSN, active evaluation acquisition, and amortized evaluation. Other entries retain their arXiv citation format; this does not assert that they have no later conference publication.

## Use

Copy the needed entries into the manuscript's bibliography, or load this file as an independent bibliography. It overlaps with `paper/neurips_2026/references.bib` (for example, amortized evaluation), so do not load both wholesale without deduplicating papers and keys. Adding a paper to this library does not add a citation to the manuscript automatically.
